    About

    From the lab bench to the whole system

    InnoNik was founded in 2025 by Naghmeh Melve, a biomedical laboratory scientist with a master's degree in interdisciplinary health research from the University of Oslo.

    Naghmeh has worked in hospital laboratories, including the emergency department, with responsibility for pre-analytics, sample reception and supervision of students. She has many years of project leadership, and works on AI-based health and research projects with international partners.

    That combination means InnoNik understands both the details of daily clinical work and the bigger picture of quality, training and technology.
